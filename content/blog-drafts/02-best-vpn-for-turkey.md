## Troubleshooting: what to do if your VPN will not connect in Turkey

If your VPN feels slow or struggles to connect in Turkey, a few simple steps usually help.

Try a different server city. If Frankfurt is slow, try Amsterdam, or another nearby European location. Network paths between Turkey and different European cities can vary.

Reconnect the app. Disconnect fully, wait a few seconds, and connect again. This clears up many temporary issues.

Check the underlying network. If hotel Wi-Fi is unstable or blocking certain traffic, try mobile data, or vice versa.

Restart the device. It is unglamorous, but a clean restart resolves a surprising number of connection issues.

Keep the app up to date. Updates from the official app stores often include reliability improvements. Installing through the App Store or Google Play keeps this simple.

If you tried these steps and still have trouble, reach out to support through the app rather than guessing at advanced settings.
